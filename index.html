About

A bit of my story

MA

I'm a machine learning engineer currently at Thomson Reuters Labs, where I build MAIA, a human-in-the-loop agent that analyzes complex M&A data rooms and produces evidence-based, traceable recommendations — plus a validation and verification layer for Westlaw's deep research agent.

Before that, I spent three years at Telus building LLM-powered enterprise search, laying the foundations for promoting ML pipelines from development to production on GCP, and shipping a graph-based recommendation system that was demoed at Google's Toronto office.

My path here started somewhere unexpected — mechanical engineering — before an MSc in Computer Science pulled me toward deep learning, and I've stayed there since. Lately I've been doing independent research on graph neural networks for recommendation, currently under review at WSDM 2026.

Outside of work I get lost in open source rabbit holes, usually with a JaBig mix playing in the background.
